# Quick Task 260908-lk6: EAN-8 and UPC-A accepted like EAN-13

## Why

Offer codes on prod `.no` (`lovata_shopaholic_offers`, `deleted_at IS NULL`, digits only), counted 2026-09-08:

| Length | Rows | Active |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | 4774 | 654 |
| 13 | 1325 | 982 |
| 12 | 96 | 7 |
| 10 | 4 | 2 |

EAN-8 is the catalog majority. The "exactly 13 digits" rule shut 654 active offers out of goods receiving.

## Locked decisions

- Regex becomes `/^(\d{8}|\d{12}|\d{13})$/` in both `HtmInvoiceParser::EAN_REGEX` and `EanMatcherService::EAN_REGEX`. Nothing else is added.
- UPC-A stays a 12-character string. No normalisation to EAN-13. The catalog stores the 12-character value, a padded value would never match, and silent rewriting is a tiger-style violation.
- Lengths 10, 15, 16 and non-digit codes stay rejected.
- Skip-and-continue in the parser (D-16) and throw-before-query in the matcher are preserved unchanged.
- No migration: `invoice_lines.ean` is `string(13)` and `InvoiceLine::$rules` says `max:13`, both already fit 8 and 12.
- Docblocks describing the old rule are rewritten, not appended to.
